Case Study: The "Fake Payment Request" UPI Scam

UPI/Online Payment Fraud is a type of cybercrime where scammers trick people into sending money or sharing their banking details. These scams often happen through calls, messages, or fake payment requests that look real. College students are common targets because they frequently use digital payment apps.

Step-by-Step Process:

The Bait: The scammer sends a message or calls the student pretending to be from a bank, online store, or even a friend. They may say there is a refund, reward, or urgent payment needed.

The Hook: The scammer sends a fake payment request or a link and tells the student to accept it or enter their UPI PIN to receive money.

The Trick: The student believes the message and enters their UPI PIN or approves the payment request without checking properly.

The Theft: As soon as the student enters the PIN or approves the request, money is transferred from their account to the scammer’s account.

Target and Consequences:

College students are often targeted because they use apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm regularly. If they are not careful, they can easily fall into such traps. The consequences include loss of money, stress, and difficulty in recovering the amount. In some cases, students may also lose trust in online payment systems.

This case study shows that users should always verify payment requests and never share their UPI PIN with anyone.
