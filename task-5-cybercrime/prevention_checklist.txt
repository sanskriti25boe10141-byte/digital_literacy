**Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist**

- Always verify unknown links, emails, or messages before clicking on them.  
- Do not share personal information like passwords, OTPs, or bank details with anyone.  
- Use strong and unique passwords for different accounts.  
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible for extra security.  
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for accessing sensitive accounts like banking apps.  
- Keep your devices and apps updated to protect against security threats.  

**Financial / UPI Safety:**
- Never share your UPI PIN with anyone, even if they claim to be from the bank.  
- Always check payment requests carefully — you enter PIN only to send money, not to receive it.
