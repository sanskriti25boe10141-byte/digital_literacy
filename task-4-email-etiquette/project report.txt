During this task, I learned the importance of professional communication, especially in emails. Poor digital communication can lead to serious problems. For example, a student once emailed a teacher regarding their grade using informal language like “Hey,” “u,” and “nvm,” without a proper subject line or greeting. Due to the unprofessional tone, the teacher did not respond, and the student missed the opportunity to clarify their grade.

This situation highlights how improper communication can negatively impact academic outcomes. It could have been avoided by using a clear subject line such as “Grade Query – [Name],” a respectful greeting, and polite, formal language.

This task taught me that effective communication and maintaining professionalism online are essential. As a Student Digital Ambassador, I understand the importance of guiding others to communicate responsibly and maintain a positive digital presence.
