Digital Literacy Awareness

This infographic is created to spread awareness about digital literacy. 
Digital literacy means the ability to use digital tools and technologies effectively and safely.

The infographic highlights important aspects such as:
- Understanding digital tools and communication
- Using strong passwords and staying safe online
- Avoiding unknown links and protecting personal data
- Maintaining a professional presence online

The aim of this project is to educate people about responsible and safe use of the internet in daily life.
